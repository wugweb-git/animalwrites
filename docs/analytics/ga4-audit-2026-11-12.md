## Totals

22,277 users, 27,336 sessions, 69% engaged, 3 min 18 s per session, 43,426 page views, 19,930 key events.
India 94% of sessions; mobile 86% (engagement 70% on mobile, 62% on desktop).

## What the numbers say

1. **One campaign carried the month.** The Lab rules campaign page was the landing page of 14,108 sessions (52%),
   and Home of 5,160 more. Daily sessions peaked at 4,032 on 4 Nov and fell to 300–400 a day after 6 Nov, below the
   500–600 a day before the campaign. The site has no steady traffic of its own yet.
2. **Channels.** Organic Search 11,200 (41%), Direct 11,031 (40%), Organic Social 4,285 (16%, nearly all Instagram),
   AI assistants 457 (ChatGPT 436, Gemini 21), Referral 218, Email 6. Bing sends 128.
   Direct is unusually high: most of it is links shared in WhatsApp and other apps, which carry no source.
3. **Key events are mostly the campaign.** objection_sent 9,788 and newsletter_optin 6,858 make up 84% of key events.
   newsletter_optin fires with an objection, so the total counts many visitors twice; read objection_sent on its own.
4. **Objection funnel.** campaign_view 20,439 → letter_open 9,796 → objection_sent 10,365 → objection_confirmed
   2,313. About 22% of senders came back to confirm.
5. **Library PDF page converts poorly.** /library/draft-laboratory-animals-rules-2026/ had 1,271 landing sessions
   (851 from Google), 56% engaged, but only 51 key events: readers of the rules rarely reached the objection.
6. **Guides do work in search.** The rule-by-rule guide (430), animal testing laws (277), how to object (186, 76%
   engaged), CPCSEA full form (88) and 1962 helpline (85) are the pages Google sends people to besides the campaign.
7. **Returning visitors are few:** 3,542 sessions (13%), 45% engaged.
8. **Data quality.**
   - `(not set)` landing page: 866 sessions at 11% engagement (sessions with no page view, mostly a session
     restarting on an event after 30 minutes idle). Unassigned channel 94.
   - 404s: test addresses (/no-such-page/), the encoded `#how-to-help` address (redirected 3 Nov), and two removed
     News items still linked somewhere (niagara-falls cat, Chamba cattle).
   - no_results: 408 of 530 are filtered Directory lists that came back empty (no search term), and the searched
     terms are mostly people's names and PIN codes.
   - Before the 12 Nov fix, the InsightWeb Pages tab showed no GA4 sessions for any page but / (path slash mismatch).

## Recommended next steps (in order)

1. **Keep the Lab campaign audience.** Point the Lab page (Post) and the Library PDF page at the next live campaign and
   the newsletter; 6,858 newsletter opt-ins are the largest owned audience the site has.
2. **Put the objection/next action on the Library PDF page** (a short box above the PDF) — 1,271 sessions, 51 key events.
3. **Tag shared links** so Direct shrinks: share buttons and WhatsApp links get `utm_source=whatsapp` (or the network)
   and `utm_medium=social`; email links get UTM.
4. **Grow search traffic beyond one campaign:** the indexing work (internal links, News sitemap, IndexNow),
   then write guides on what people already find: draft-rule how-tos, animal law explainers, helplines.
5. **Watch AI assistants** (457 sessions, growing): keep the guides' FAQ and schema current; check InsightWeb's AI
   column weekly.
6. **Bing:** 128 sessions; IndexNow and Bing Webmaster checks.
7. **Key events hygiene:** consider unmarking newsletter_optin as a key event (keep it as an event), or read reports
   by event, so the total is not doubled.
8. **Confirm rate:** test a clearer confirm step for objections (22% confirmed).
9. **Fix the two dead News links** (find the pages linking to them) and leave test 404s alone.
10. **Connect GA4 in OpenSEO** (owner, Settings → Integrations) so its GA4 tools can be used next time.
11. **Clarity, 19 Nov:** compare dead clicks on the Library PDF page before and after the 12 Nov button change; Clarity
    now also receives pdf_download as a custom event.

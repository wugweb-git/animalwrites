Every draft has:

- a focus keyword plus two secondaries, and a primary category;

## 1. Audience and positioning

- **Readers:** people in India who are dealing with an animal problem right now (an injured animal, a cruelty case, a feeding dispute), plus people looking up a law, a rule, a body or a date. Organisations and volunteers use the Directory and Opportunities.
- **What we do differently:** we are the plain-English layer over primary sources. We summarise News with the publisher credited, keep Library documents in full with the official PDF, write guides that answer one question first, and run a Directory of 5,139 listings.
- **Brand vs non-brand:**
  - Non-brand search is still small: about 4,600 impressions and 78 clicks in 11 months.
  - It lands mostly on Directory place pages (position about 8) and three guides (ABC, reporting cruelty, injured animal, at about position 5).
  - New content should go where people already search and nobody owns the answer, not after head terms.

#### Read this first: mobile lab runs were bimodal today

Each mobile run fell into one of two modes:

- **fast:** observed first paint at about 0.5–1.4 s;
- **stalled:** observed first paint fixed at **about 2.33–2.46 s**, even though the HTML, CSS, fonts and the LCP image had all finished by about 0.8 s.

**What the trace shows for a stalled run** (news item, saved with `--save-assets`):
- the main thread is idle between about 1.3 s and 2.37 s;
- no frames are produced in that window;
- the page then paints everything at once.

The stall still happened with each of these changes:
- with the new azurefd.net "brand-agent" script blocked: 3 of 5 runs stalled;
- with cross-document view transitions turned off in Chrome: 3 of 6 stalled;
- with `--disable-gpu`: 4 of 6 stalled.

Paint times land in steps of about 1 s (about 0.55, 1.38 and 2.38 s). That points to how this headless Chrome schedules frames on this machine, not to site code. However, it was not proven either way.

- Chrome updated itself to 155.0.8059.85 at 11:31 on 20 Oct.
- The 18 Oct runs on this machine showed observed FCP of 1.2–1.4 s.

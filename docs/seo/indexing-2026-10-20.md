# Indexing: Directory, 20 October 2026

Search Console's biggest "not indexed" reason is "Discovered – currently not indexed", with 6,044 pages. Google knows these URLs but has not fetched them yet. Validation was started on 13 Oct and is still pending, with 0 failed.

## What the audit found

- **Which pages are waiting:** in the 1,000 example URLs, 63% are organisation profiles (/directory/org/), 24% are district or district × type pages, 9% are News and the rest are Library, Resources and Opportunities.
- **How fast Google crawls:** 6.68K requests in 90 days, about 74 a day. Two thirds of those are HTML and 41% are discovery, so roughly 20 new URLs a day. The sitemaps list about 10,200 URLs, 8,630 of them in the Directory.
- **Speed is not the blocker:** Google's average response time is 506 ms. LiteSpeed answers a cached page in 9 ms and builds an uncached one in about 0.44 s. robots.txt and canonicals are correct, and every page sampled was 200 with index,follow.
- **Thin list pages:** of the 1,956 district × type pages, 1,224 list one organisation, 202 list two, 107 list three, 70 list four, and 353 list five or more. A one-organisation page mostly repeats that profile.
- **Profile dead ends:** a profile linked only to other organisations in its own district. With one listing in the district, it had no organisation links at all.

## What changed (owner's decisions, 20 Oct)

1. **Thin pages:** a district × type page with fewer than 3 organisations is now noindex (links still followed) and out of directory-sitemap.xml. A page becomes indexable again by itself when it reaches 3. See `aw-seo-extras/includes/robots.php` (`AW_THIN_DISTRICT_TYPE = 3`).

3. **Profile links:** when a district has fewer than 6 other organisations, the profile adds a "More organisations in {State}" group that fills the list up to 6, in this order:
   - the same type in nearby districts (within 150 km);
   - other types nearby;
   - the rest of the state.

   Within each step the nearest come first, then A–Z, so the order is the same on every load. No organisation appears twice. District centres are in `animal-writes-core/data/district-centroids.json` (from OpenStreetMap Nominatim).

### 1. 127 How to report animal cruelty
- Picture: text card on the brand template (black, white, greys).
- Card (approved): "Saw cruelty to an animal? / Report it at any police station. / If they refuse: write to the SP, then the magistrate. / animalwrites.in"
- Caption (approved): Saw someone hurt an animal? You can report it at any police station in India, wherever it happened. That is a Zero FIR, under section 173(1) of the BNSS. If the police refuse, send your complaint by post to the Superintendent of Police. If that fails, go to the magistrate with an affidavit. You can also complain online to the Animal Welfare Board of India. The full steps, state by state, are on our guide. Link in bio. #AnimalWrites #AnimalCruelty #ReportCruelty #FIR #AnimalLawIndia

### 2. Animal cruelty law in India (new page)
- Picture: text card on the brand template (black, white, greys).
- Card (approved): "Hurting an animal in India: / Rs 10 to Rs 50 under the PCA Act. / Killing or maiming one: up to 5 years (BNS 325). / animalwrites.in"
- Caption (approved): The fine for cruelty to an animal in India is still Rs 10 to Rs 50 for a first offence (PCA Act, section 11). The 2022 bill to raise it has never reached Parliament. Killing, poisoning or maiming an animal is different: up to five years in prison under section 325 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, and the police can register it without a magistrate's order. Every offence and penalty, in plain words, on our new guide. Link in bio. #AnimalWrites #AnimalCruelty #PCAAct #BNS325 #AnimalLawIndia

### 3. 9029 Feeding stray dogs and the law
- Picture: text card on the brand template (black, white, greys).
- Card (approved): "Feeding stray dogs in India: / Not on the street. / Only at a designated feeding spot. / Supreme Court, 22 Aug 2025 / animalwrites.in"
- Caption (approved): Can you still feed community dogs in India? Yes, but only at a designated feeding spot. Since the Supreme Court's order of 22 Aug 2025, feeding on the streets is not allowed anywhere in India. Municipal bodies must set up feeding spaces in every ward. In a housing society, your RWA must agree spots and times with feeders under rule 20 of the ABC Rules 2023, and it has no power to ban feeding or fine you. What the law says, place by place, on our guide. Link in bio. #AnimalWrites #CommunityDogs #StrayDogs #ABCRules #SupremeCourt

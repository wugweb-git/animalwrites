# India's 1962 animal helpline and Mobile Veterinary Units (MVUs), as of 18 Nov 2026

Research note (18 Nov 2026). Access limits: pib.gov.in, dahd.gov.in, deccanherald.com returned HTTP 403/404 to the fetch tool, and eparlib.sansad.in refused connections. Where a figure comes only from a search-result summary of those pages (not a full read), it is marked "(search summary only)". The EMRI GHS December 2025 report was read in full as a PDF.

## 1. What is 1962, who runs it, funding

### Takeaway
1962 is a toll-free veterinary helpline. Each State/UT runs its own call centre under the central component "Establishment & Strengthening of Veterinary Hospitals & Dispensaries – Mobile Veterinary Units" (ESVHD-MVU). That component sits in DAHD's Livestock Health & Disease Control scheme, now the Livestock Health and Disease Control Programme (LHDCP). The Centre pays 100% of the vehicle cost. Running costs are shared: 60:40 in most states, 90:10 in NE/Himalayan states, 100% central in UTs. Several states outsource operations, mostly to EMRI Green Health Services, and in Rajasthan to a CSR-funding company.

### Cited findings
- PIB, 8 May 2022 (Karnataka MVU flag-off): the minister urged farmers and dairy owners to use 290 MVUs by calling "Toll free Animal Helpline Number 1962". The component is ESVHD-MVU under the Central Sector scheme "Livestock Health & Disease Control", giving veterinary services at farmers' doorsteps. Calls go to a call centre, emergencies are prioritised, and a veterinarian plus para-vet team diagnoses and treats. — [PIB PRID 1823690](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1823690) (search summary only)
- Funding: the Centre pays 100% for buying and fitting out the MVUs. Running costs are 60:40 Centre:State for general states, 90:10 for NE and Himalayan states, 100% central for UTs. A 2022 release gave the running cost as Rs 18.72 lakh per vehicle per year, shared 60:40. — [DAHD Operational Guidelines for LHDCP (2025-05)](https://dahd.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-05/OperationalGuidelinesforLHDCP.pdf); [PIB PRID 1823690](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1823690) (search summary only; the guidelines PDF itself returned 403/404)
- Norm: about 1 MVU per 1 lakh livestock. Crew: a veterinary doctor, a livestock inspector (para-vet) and a driver-cum-attendant. 1962 is a State/UT-level call centre that triages urgency and arranges follow-up. — [DAHD LHDCP Operational Guidelines](https://dahd.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-05/OperationalGuidelinesforLHDCP.pdf); [DAHD LH&DC scheme page](https://dahd.gov.in/en/schemes-programmes/lh-dc) (search summary only)
- Call centre design: a "30 seated Call Centre" for round-the-clock service with two veterinarians, per the Vikaspedia MVU page (government portal, page body not readable). Delhi's plan: 1 veterinarian and 3 call executives in two shifts a day. — [Vikaspedia: Mobile veterinary units](https://en.vikaspedia.in/agriculture/livestock/mobile-veterinary-units); [LatestLY/PTI: Delhi govt to set up call centre](https://www.latestly.com/agency-news/latest-news-delhi-govt-to-set-up-call-centre-for-providing-veterinary-care-6172310.html) (search summary only)
- Scheme start: the MVU scheme began in 2021-22 (Lok Sabha Unstarred Q 1287, 11 Feb 2025). — [Sansad AU1287](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/2988537/1/AU1287_lO8smW.pdf) (search summary only)
- 1962 is older than the central scheme. Tamil Nadu started AMMA (Animal Medical Mobile Ambulance) on 1962 in Sept 2016 (The Hindu, 17 Nov 2016). Gujarat started Karuna Animal Ambulance on 1962 in Oct 2017. Telangana started Pashu Arogya Seva/1962 in Sept 2017 with 100 Mobile Veterinary Clinics. — [Wikipedia: 1962 (emergency telephone number)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962_(emergency_telephone_number)); [All Gujarat News, 23 Jun 2023](https://allgujaratnews.in/en/animal-emergency-ambulance/); [Newstap, Telangana 1962](https://www.newstap.in/metro-city/mankind-1962-emergency-helpline-saves-51000-animals-in-telangana-in-october-1446395) (search summary only)
- Operator: EMRI Green Health Services (EMRI GHS) runs "1962 Pashu Arogya Seva" with 1,581 vehicles in 8 states/UT, as of December 2025. — [EMRI GHS Monthly Report, Dec 2025](http://www.emri.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/EMRI-Green-Health-Services-Monthly-Report-December-25.pdf) (read in full)
- Rajasthan's 1962 call centre is run by a company that funds the scheme under CSR. — [BFIL "Bharat Sanjeevani" CSR page](https://www.bfil.co.in/csr-sanajeevani-details.php) (search summary only)
- Madhya Pradesh's earlier "Cow Express" on 1962 (announced Oct 2018) is described as out of service. — [Wikipedia: 1962](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1962_(emergency_telephone_number))

### Inferences
- "1962 helpline kiska hai?" (whose helpline is it?) The accurate short answer: it is each state's Animal Husbandry / Veterinary Department helpline, set up under the Government of India's DAHD MVU scheme (LHDCP). It is often run by a contracted operator such as EMRI GHS. It is not a private NGO line, and there is no single national call centre.
- 1962 is not a single launch event. The number was used by some states (TN 2016, Gujarat and Telangana 2017) before the 2021-22 central MVU scheme standardised it.

- No full list of operators per state. Karnataka's, Maharashtra's, Rajasthan's and others' operators are not confirmed here.
- The LH&DC to LHDCP rename date was not confirmed. The guidelines file is dated 2025-05.

Two national figures exist, both from Lok Sabha replies in 2025. In February 2025: 4,340 MVUs reported in total. In August 2025: 4,019 MVUs operational in 29 States/UTs, used through 1962. I found no 2026 national figure. Coverage is not universal: 29 States/UTs out of 36.

## 3. What an MVU does: species, services, fees

### Takeaway
MVUs are built for livestock (farmers' cattle, buffalo, small ruminants etc.). They provide on-site diagnosis, treatment, minor surgery and extension work, free of charge. Some state 1962 services go further. Gujarat's Karuna ambulance covers injured animals and birds, mostly dogs. Odisha's 1962 is for stray and abandoned animals. Whether stray dogs or pets are covered depends on the state.

### Cited findings
- Central scheme: veterinary health services at farmers' doorsteps. A vet and para-vet team does diagnosis and treatment. Callers are "farmers" / "needy livestock farmers". — [PIB PRID 1823690](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=1823690); [Sansad AU3885](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/2995631/1/AU3885_2lUdSR.pdf) (search summaries only)
- Gujarat Karuna Animal Ambulance-1962 (since Oct 2017): "free treatment of injured and sick animals and birds on the spot". Timings 8 AM to 8 PM. Dogs are the largest group: about 67,000 dogs treated, mostly road accidents. 1.72 lakh calls from Oct 2017 to Nov 2019, with 1.10 lakh animals and birds treated. The article says about 40% of calls went untreated. — [All Gujarat News, 23 Jun 2023](https://allgujaratnews.in/en/animal-emergency-ambulance/)
- Gujarat Karuna Abhiyan (annual Uttarayan kite-string bird rescue, 10–20 Jan, running since 2015). 1962 is the Animal Husbandry helpline for injured animals and birds. The Forest Department uses 1926 and WhatsApp 8320002000 (send "Karuna"). — [CMO Gujarat](https://cmogujarat.gov.in/en/latest-news/statewide-karuna-abhiyan-launched-in-gujarat-aiming-at-saving-birds-injured-by-kite-string/); [DeshGujarat, 14 Jan 2025](https://deshgujarat.com/2025/01/14/uttarayan-2025-bird-rescue-helpline-and-whatsapp-numbers-in-gujarat/); [DeshGujarat, 13 Jan 2026](https://deshgujarat.com/2026/01/13/karuna-abhiyan-injured-bird-helpline-number-released-for-kite-flying-festival-in-gujarat/) (search summaries only; the 2026 numbers were not read in full)
- Odisha 1962 (launched 7 Jun 2022 by CM Naveen Patnaik): State Animal Helpline for "stray and abandoned animals". Runs 24x7. Free veterinary service through animal ambulances in 11 urban areas. Tele-veterinary advice for farmers on 1962 or 155333. — [Sambad English, 7 Jun 2022](https://sambadenglish.com/odisha-animal-helpline-1962-launched-by-cm-naveen-patnaik/); [OdishaTV](https://odishatv.in/news/miscellaneous/odisha-launches-animal-helpline-tele-veterinary-services-178479)
- Bhubaneswar Municipal Corporation (BMC) answered a citizen on X: "The animal helpline number is 1962". — [BMC on X](https://x.com/bmcbbsr/status/1873285692102738328?lang=en)
- EMRI GHS describes its services (108, 1962 etc.) as "free". — [EMRI GHS Dec 2025 report](http://www.emri.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/EMRI-Green-Health-Services-Monthly-Report-December-25.pdf)

### Inferences
- A public page should not say "1962 picks up stray dogs everywhere". A safe wording: "a livestock vet service in most states; some states (e.g. Gujarat, Odisha) also attend injured strays and birds."
- I found no source for vaccination or artificial insemination (AI) as standard MVU services. Leave them out unless a guideline text confirms them.

### Gaps
- The exact service list in the LHDCP MVU guidelines (vaccination? AI? surgery?) could not be read.
- No confirmed central rule on user fees. The services are described as free in state sources.

## 4. Hours

### Takeaway
Call centres are generally 24x7 (Karnataka, Odisha, and the central design of "round-the-clock"). Vehicles run day shifts that vary by state: Karnataka 9 am–5 pm, Gujarat Karuna 8 am–8 pm.

### Cited findings
- Karnataka: call centre 24/7, ambulances 9 am to 5 pm. — [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/india/karnataka/bengaluru/state-s-1962-helpline-treats-5-lakh-animals-in-19-months-3444517) (search summary only)
- Gujarat Karuna: 8 AM to 8 PM. — [All Gujarat News, 23 Jun 2023](https://allgujaratnews.in/en/animal-emergency-ambulance/)
- Odisha helpline: 24x7. Tele-vet reported as 10 am–5:30 pm in one source. — [Sambad English](https://sambadenglish.com/odisha-animal-helpline-1962-launched-by-cm-naveen-patnaik/); [Kalinga TV](https://kalingatv.com/odisha/odisha-cm-launched-animal-helpline-number-for-protection-of-animals-details-here/) (the tele-vet hours are from a search summary only)
- Central design: a round-the-clock call centre. — [Vikaspedia](https://en.vikaspedia.in/agriculture/livestock/mobile-veterinary-units) (search summary only)

### Gaps
- No central rule found on MVU operating hours.

## 5. Relationship to 112 (ERSS)

### Takeaway
I found no source showing that 112 (ERSS) handles animal emergencies or is integrated with 1962. They are separate numbers. 1962 runs through state animal husbandry call centres. 112 is the police/fire/ambulance emergency number for people.

### Cited findings
- No official source found linking 112 ERSS to animal calls.
- EMRI GHS runs 108 (human ambulance) and 1962 as separate lines in the same states. — [EMRI GHS Dec 2025 report](http://www.emri.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/EMRI-Green-Health-Services-Monthly-Report-December-25.pdf)
- Note: the search returned Animal Writes' own page ("call 1962 … call 112 if people are in danger"), animalwrites.in/resources/found-an-injured-animal-what-to-do/. It is the page being checked, so it is not counted as evidence.

### Inferences
- Wording that stays accurate: "Call 112 only if people are at risk (e.g. an animal causing a road hazard); for the animal, call 1962 where your state runs it."

### Gaps
- No MHA/ERSS SOP read on animal-related calls.

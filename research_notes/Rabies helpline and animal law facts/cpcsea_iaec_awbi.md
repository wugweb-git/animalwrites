# CPCSEA/CCSEA, IAEC and AWBI: facts as of November 2026

Pages read 18 Nov 2026 unless another date is given.

## CPCSEA / CCSEA: name, rename, ministry, legal basis, contact, registration

### Takeaway
CPCSEA is now called CCSEA (Committee for Control and Supervision of Experiments on Animals). It is a statutory committee under the PCA Act 1960, sitting in the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD), Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying. The rename was made by a ministry order of 6 Jan 2023 (news reports). The official website is ccsea.gov.in. The 1998 Rules (amended 2001 and 2006) are still the operative rules. The official contact email is still cpcsea-mef[at]gov[dot]in, and CCSEA's own Contact page says email is the preferred channel.

### Cited Findings
- Old full name: "Committee for the Purpose of Control and Supervision of Experiments on Animals (CPCSEA)". New name: "Committee for Control and Supervision of Experiments on Animals (CCSEA)". It is "a statutory Committee of Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying (DAHD), Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying (MoFAH&D) constituted under the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals (PCA) Act, 1960". — [ICMR, FAQ for Institutional Animal Ethics Committees, 2025, Q.1](https://icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/Guidelines/1744029514_final_faq_iaecapproval1_1.pdf)
- Rename: an MoFAH&D order of 6 Jan 2023 renamed CPCSEA to CCSEA. The aim was a shorter title, and the committee's powers did not change. — [Pashu Sandesh, 12 Jan 2023](https://pashusandesh.com/The-name-of-CPCSEA-changed-to-CCSEA); [Pashudhan Praharee](https://pashudhanpraharee.com/?p=21483). These are secondary sources. I did not find the order itself or a gazette entry for it.
- Objective: "to ensure that animals are not subjected to unnecessary pain or suffering before, during or after the performance of experiments on them." — [ICMR FAQ 2025, Q.2](https://icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/Guidelines/1744029514_final_faq_iaecapproval1_1.pdf)
- Official website: ccsea.gov.in, run by DAHD, MoFAH&D. The page was "Last Updated On 15 Oct 2026". It names the Chairman as Dr. Muthukumarasamy B., Joint Secretary (Animal Welfare), and the Member Secretary as Dr. S. K. Dutta, Joint Commissioner (DAHD). It says the present CCSEA has 19 members. The browser title still reads "Committee for the Purpose of Control And Supervision...", so the old wording survives there. — [ccsea.gov.in](https://ccsea.gov.in)
- Rules: under the Breeding of and Experiments on Animals (Control & Supervision) Rules, 1998 (amended 2001 and 2006), establishments doing biomedical research, breeding or trading of lab animals must register with CCSEA. — [ccsea.gov.in](https://ccsea.gov.in). The original rules are G.S.R. 1074(E), 15 Dec 1998. — [animallaw.info](https://www.animallaw.info/node/41676)
- Legal basis: the request names PCA Act s.15 as the section that sets up the committee. I did not open the Act text to confirm the section number. The ICMR FAQ says only "constituted under the PCA Act, 1960".
- Registration rules in the 1998 text:
  - Rule 4(a): "No establishment shall perform any experiment on animals unless it is registered."
  - Rule 3(a): breeding or trading animals for experiments also needs registration.
  - Rule 5(a): applications go "in the specified format" to the Member-Secretary or an authorised officer.
  - Rule 8(a): a registered establishment must get permission from "the Committee or the Institutional Animals Ethics Committee" before acquiring animals or experimenting on them.
  - Source: [animallaw.info, 1998 text](https://www.animallaw.info/node/41600)
- Registration process today: the ccsea.gov.in portal handles:
  - New Registration (with a user manual)
  - Renewal of Registration / Reconstitution of IAEC
  - Revision of IAEC
  - submission of IAEC minutes, inspection reports and research protocols
  - Amendment of Registration
  - Change Nominee
  - Fees are paid through a payment gateway and are "not ... refunded under any circumstances".
  - Source: [ccsea.gov.in](https://ccsea.gov.in)
- Contact, quoted from CCSEA's Contact page:
  - Chairman: Room No. 190-A, Krishi Bhawan, New Delhi 110001. Tel 011-23382354. Email js-nlm[at]dahd[dot]nic[dot]in.
  - Member Secretary: 2nd Floor, Chanderlok, 36 Janpath, New Delhi 110001. Tel/Fax 011-21401454. Email sk[dot]dutta[at]nic[dot]in.
  - Correspondence address: Delhi Milk Scheme Complex, near State Bank of India, Shadipur, Delhi 110008. Tel 011-20838262. Email cpcsea-mef[at]gov[dot]in.
  - The page says: "Enquiries through email (cpcsea-mef[at]gov[dot]in) will be prefered". Phone hours are 10:00 to 17:00.
  - Source: [ccsea.gov.in Contact Us](https://ccsea.gov.in/Content/56_1_CONTACTUS.aspx)
- Draft rules, one line: the draft rules G.S.R. 864(E) were issued 6 Oct 2026. Their full title is the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals (Control and Supervision of Experiment, Breeding and Trading of Laboratory Animals) Rules, 2026. If finalised, they would replace the 1998 Rules (and, per ThePrint, the 1968 Rules). They are still a draft. — [MedBound Times](https://www.medboundtimes.com/daily-pulse/gsr-864-e-draft-animal-experimentation-rules-2026-objections); [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/animal-rights-activists-oppose-draft-rules-on-lab-experiments-seek-stronger-safeguards/3092408/); [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/articles/self-approval-another-name-2026draft-animal-testing-rules-pca-act-556696)

### Inferences
- An explainer can safely say "CCSEA (formerly CPCSEA)". The name CPCSEA is out of date for current use but is still used informally and in many institutional forms. CPCSEA-era guideline PDFs and institutional forms (Form B etc.) are old copies.
- cpcsea-mef@gov.in is current. It is the only general enquiry address CCSEA publishes. I found no newer address such as an @ccsea.gov.in one.
- The Chairman's email "js-nlm" is a role address. Officer names (Dr. Muthukumarasamy B., Dr. S.K. Dutta) can change, so an explainer should not hard-code them, or should date them.

## IAEC: full form, composition, what it approves, when CCSEA approval is needed

### Takeaway
IAEC means Institutional Animal Ethics Committee. In the 1998 Rules it is spelled "Institutional Animals Ethics Committee". An IAEC is set up and registered by CCSEA for 5 years and has 5 institution members plus 4 CCSEA nominees. It can approve work on animals up to the level of rodents, including rabbits and birds. Work on dogs, goats, pigs, cattle, monkeys and other animals higher than rodents needs CCSEA approval, sent through the IAEC with its recommendation.

### Cited Findings
- Definition: a body "recognized and registered by the CCSEA for the purpose of control and supervision of experiments on animals performed in an establishment". — [ICMR FAQ 2025, Q.3](https://icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/Guidelines/1744029514_final_faq_iaecapproval1_1.pdf)
- Composition per the ICMR FAQ 2025, Q.4 ([source](https://icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/Guidelines/1744029514_final_faq_iaecapproval1_1.pdf)):
  - Members from the institution:
    - a Biological Scientist
    - two Scientists from different biological disciplines
    - a veterinarian involved in the care of animals (mandatory)
    - the Scientist in charge of the Animal House Facility
  - The Chairman (preferably the Head of the Institution or Department) and the Member Secretary are picked from these five.
  - Members nominated by CCSEA: the Main Nominee, the Link Nominee, a Scientist from outside the Institute, and the Socially Aware Nominee.
  - CCSEA constitutes the IAEC at registration for 5 years. At least half the members must be replaced at each reconstitution.
- 1998 Rule 13 has the same list: a biological scientist, two scientists from different biological disciplines, a veterinarian, the scientist in charge of the animal facility, a scientist from outside, a non-scientific socially aware member, and a nominee of the Committee. It adds that "a specialist may be co-opted" for projects that use hazardous agents. — [animallaw.info](https://www.animallaw.info/node/41600)
- Example roster: IMTECH lists CPCSEA main, link and socially aware nominees. The link nominee stands in when the main nominee is unavailable. — [IMTECH IAEC page](https://imtech.res.in/right-to-information/institutional-animal-ethics-committee) (undated)
- Conflicting counts: institutional SOPs give 8, 7 or 9 members. — [scribd SOP](https://www.scribd.com/document/448560099/ANIMAL-ETHICS-SOP-pdf). The ICMR FAQ lists 9 roles: 5 institutional plus 4 CCSEA nominees.
- What an IAEC approves: "IAEC is authorized to approve experiments involving animals up to the phylogenetic level of rodents. Thus rats, mice, birds, and rabbits are also subject to IAEC approval." For animals higher than rodents, "IAEC can provide recommendations and forward the proposal to the CCSEA for approval." — [ICMR FAQ 2025, Q.7](https://icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/Guidelines/1744029514_final_faq_iaecapproval1_1.pdf)
- Large animals: for "dogs, goats, pigs, cattle, monkeys, etc" the protocol must go to CCSEA with the IAEC's recommendation, and may also need rodent study data. — [ICMR FAQ 2025, Q.9](https://icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/Guidelines/1744029514_final_faq_iaecapproval1_1.pdf)
- Exception: small blood draws from large animals (up to 0.6 ml/kg per week) for feeding, breeding, livestock, diagnosis, field or thesis studies may be approved by the IAEC alone. This applies at ICMR, ICAR, veterinary and agriculture universities, and government-funded institutions. — [ICMR FAQ 2025, Q.13](https://icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/Guidelines/1744029514_final_faq_iaecapproval1_1.pdf)
- Procedure:
  - The Principal Investigator submits Form B to the IAEC.
  - IAEC approval lasts one year. In-principle approval for longer is possible, with yearly progress review.
  - Invertebrates need no regulation.
  - Work on wild animals may also need clearance from the state Chief Wild Life Warden or MoEFCC.
  - Source: [ICMR FAQ 2025, Q.6, Q.8, Q.10](https://icmr.gov.in/icmrobject/uploads/Guidelines/1744029514_final_faq_iaecapproval1_1.pdf)

### Inferences
- The rodent / non-rodent split is CCSEA practice set out in guidance. The 1998 Rule 8 text itself does not split approvals by species. — [animallaw.info](https://www.animallaw.info/node/41600). An explainer should say "under CCSEA guidelines".
- The 2026 draft would move more approvals to the IAEC alone. That makes the current split the rule "as of Nov 2026, pending new rules".

## AWBI: full form, founding, headquarters, ministry, functions, chair, website, AWO recognition, complaints

### Takeaway
AWBI means Animal Welfare Board of India. It is a statutory advisory body set up in 1962 under Section 4 of the PCA Act 1960, within DAHD, MoFAH&D. Its headquarters moved from Chennai to Ballabhgarh, Faridabad, Haryana in early 2018. Its Chairman since 1 Oct 2025 is Dr. Muthukumarasamy B., IAS, Joint Secretary, DAHD, who also chairs CCSEA. Its website is awbi.gov.in.

### Cited Findings
- Established in 1962 under Section 4 of the PCA Act 1960. It is a statutory advisory body on animal welfare laws: it works to ensure those laws are followed, gives grants to AWOs, and advises the Government of India. — [awbi.gov.in](https://awbi.gov.in)
- Ministry: MoFAH&D (DAHD). — [awbi.gov.in](https://awbi.gov.in)
- Headquarters: 42 Milestone, Delhi-Agra Highway, NH-2, Village Sikri (Seekri), Ballabhgarh, Faridabad, Haryana 121004. Tel 0129-2555700, Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:30. — [awbi.gov.in](https://awbi.gov.in)
- The move to Ballabhgarh was disclosed in the government's Rajya Sabha reply of 5 Mar 2018. The stated reason was better coordination with the ministry. — [Business Standard / PTI, 5 Mar 2018](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/awbi-headquarters-shifted-to-haryana-118030501175_1.html); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_Welfare_Board_of_India). Some study guides still say Chennai. That is OUTDATED.
- Chairman: an AWBI circular (No. 11-10/2019-20/Estt.) says the Central Government nominated "Dr. Muthukumarasamy B., IAS, Joint Secretary, Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying, as the Chairman, Animal Welfare Board of India, with effect from 01.10.2025". It cites S.O. 4500(E) of 01.10.2025, and S.O. 4376(E) and 4377(E) of 22.09.2025. The board itself was reconstituted by S.O. 2222(E) of 15 May 2023, as amended. — [AWBI circular PDF](https://awbi.gov.in/uploads/circular/177064141696Nomination%20of%20Chairman%20and%20change%20in%20Membership.pdf). The homepage also lists him as Chairman. — [awbi.gov.in](https://awbi.gov.in)
- Email: the homepage gives support@awbi.gov.in and support-awbi@awbi.gov.in. — [awbi.gov.in](https://awbi.gov.in). The circular letterhead gives support-awbi@gov.in. — [AWBI circular](https://awbi.gov.in/uploads/circular/177064141696Nomination%20of%20Chairman%20and%20change%20in%20Membership.pdf). The two sources disagree. Use the one shown on the homepage, or check before publishing.
- AWO recognition:
  - AWBI recognises organisations and publishes a "List of Organizations".
  - Recognition lasts five years.
  - AWOs whose recognition had lapsed had to apply online for renewal, with the fee, by 9 Oct 2026, or face derecognition.
  - Source: [awbi.gov.in](https://awbi.gov.in)
- Complaints:
  - Online Services has "Grievance" and "Cruelty" pages for grievances and cruelty reports.
  - AWBI warns about fake letters that misuse its name and says to verify them through its official email.
  - Source: [awbi.gov.in](https://awbi.gov.in)

### Inferences
- AWBI and CCSEA currently share the same chair, the DAHD Joint Secretary (Animal Welfare). Any explainer naming a chair should date the claim, for example "as of Nov 2026".

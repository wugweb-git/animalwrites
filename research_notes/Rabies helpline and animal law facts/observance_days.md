# Four animal observance days for India-focused pages (2027 refresh; checked 18 Nov 2026)

## International Tiger Day (Global Tiger Day), 29 July: origin, India's tiger figures, NTCA

### Takeaway
29 July is set out in the St Petersburg Declaration (Tiger Summit, 21–24 Nov 2010). India's latest official national figure is still the 2022 All India Tiger Estimation: an average of 3,682, in a range of 3,167–3,925. The sixth cycle (AITE 2026) began fieldwork in January 2026. NTCA's own newsletter says its final report "is expected in 2027", so no 2026 result had been released as of November 2026.

### Cited Findings
- The St Petersburg Declaration on Tiger Conservation (dated 23 Nov 2010, signed by heads of government of the 13 tiger range countries) includes a commitment to mark Global Tiger Day on 29 July every year. — [IUCN-hosted St Petersburg Declaration PDF](https://iucn.org/sites/default/files/import/downloads/st_petersburg_declaration_english.pdf) (accessed 18 Nov 2026)
- Nuance: TRAFFIC (2014) wrote that 29 July "was first declared Global Tiger Day" before the November 2010 summit, so the date may predate the summit, which then formalised it. — [TRAFFIC, Global Tiger Day 2014](https://traffic.org/news/global-tiger-day-2014-future-for-tigers-uncertain)
- The summit's TX2 goal was to double wild tiger numbers by 2022. The 13 range countries are Bangladesh, Bhutan, Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Nepal, Russia, Thailand and Viet Nam. — [TRAFFIC](https://traffic.org/news/global-tiger-day-2014-future-for-tigers-uncertain)
- AITE 2022 detailed report (PIB, MoEFCC, 29 Jul 2023): the average is 3,682 tigers and the upper limit 3,925. The earlier minimum of 3,167 (camera-trapped area) was announced by the PM at Mysuru at the 50 years of Project Tiger event. Growth is 6.1% per year. Top states: Madhya Pradesh 785, Karnataka 563, Uttarakhand 560, Maharashtra 444. India holds about 75% of the world's wild tigers. — [PIB PRID 1943922, 29 Jul 2023](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1943922)
- NTCA STRIPES newsletter (July 2026, file dated 24.07.2026): AITE 2026 is the sixth cycle. Field teams started in January 2026. It is coordinated by NTCA with WII and carried out by state forest departments. It covers 58 tiger reserves (51 in 2022) and some new landscapes. Earlier cycles were 2006, 2010, 2014, 2018 and 2022. The 2022 cycle "returned an estimate of 3,682 tigers". Quote: "The final report of the Sixth Cycle is expected in 2027." — [NTCA STRIPES July 2026 PDF](https://cache.ntca.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/STRIPES_July26_24.07.2026_compressed.pdf)
- The same newsletter carries a "Global Tiger Day 29 July 2026" page. — [NTCA STRIPES July 2026](https://cache.ntca.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/STRIPES_July26_24.07.2026_compressed.pdf)
- Only state-level preliminary AITE 2026 field data has been reported, not a national estimate. Example: Telangana logged 994 signs of tigers and other carnivores. — [Siasat](https://www.siasat.com/telangana-concludes-tiger-survey-logs-994-carnivore-signs-3332985); [Telangana Today](https://telanganatoday.com/all-india-tiger-estimation-2026-survey-completed-in-telangana)
- Release timing conflicts in the press: one Indore report expected AITE 2026 results "by July 2026", while other outlets say 2027. NTCA's own 2027 statement should be preferred. — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/indore/all-india-tiger-estimation-2026-choral-records-highest-18-tiger-pugmark-signs-mhow-follows); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/india-to-begin-sixth-cycle-of-tiger-census-report-to-be-released-in-2026/amp)

### Inferences
- Earlier cycle reports came out on or near 29 July (2019 and 2023). Going by that pattern, the AITE 2026 result is likely around Global Tiger Day, 29 July 2027 (Thursday). This is a pattern, not an announcement. Pages should keep "3,682 (2022)" until NTCA or PIB publishes.
- The 2022 report came in two steps: the minimum on 9 Apr 2023 at Mysuru, then the full report on 29 Jul 2023. A 2027 release may also come in two steps.

### Gaps
- No official release date for the AITE 2026 report has been announced.
- One aggregator dated the Mysuru 3,167 announcement "9 April 2022". The event (50 years of Project Tiger) was in April 2023. I did not open a primary page to confirm the exact day.
- ThePrint's article on the 2027 report timing was behind a bot check and could not be read.

## World Animal Day, 4 October: origin, organiser, themes

### Takeaway
World Animal Day falls on 4 October, the feast day of St Francis of Assisi. Heinrich Zimmermann started it with a first event on 24 March 1925. Naturewatch Foundation (UK) has run it since it launched the World Animal Day website in 2003. The 2026 theme was "See the World Through Their Eyes". No 2027 theme had been announced by November 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Heinrich Zimmermann, writer and publisher of the German magazine *Mensch und Hund*, held the first celebration on 24 March 1925 with over 5,000 people. 4 October is the feast day of St Francis of Assisi, patron saint of animals. In 2003, Naturewatch Foundation, a UK charity, launched the World Animal Day website. — [World Animal Day, About us](https://www.worldanimalday.org/about-us/)
- 2026 theme: "See the World Through Their Eyes", submitted by "Zoe from the UK" and announced 19 Mar 2026. The page says the previous year (2025) marked the 100th anniversary. — [World Animal Day, 2026 theme](https://www.worldanimalday.org/announcing-the-world-animal-day-2026-theme/); [Naturewatch](https://naturewatch.org/the-world-animal-day-2026-theme/)
- Conflict: one awareness-days calendar gives the 2026 theme as "Great or Small, Love Them All". This contradicts both official pages and looks like an error. — [awarenessdays.com](https://www.awarenessdays.com/awareness-days-calendar/world-animal-day-2023/)
- 4 October 2026 was a Sunday. — [World Animal Day site](https://www.worldanimalday.org/announcing-the-world-animal-day-2026-theme/)

### Inferences
- 4 October 2027 is a Monday (calendar arithmetic). The 2026 theme was announced in March, so a 2027 theme can be expected around March 2027.

### Gaps
- The often-cited details (first event in Berlin, the 1929 move to 4 October, the 1931 Florence congress adopting it) were not on the official About page. They are unverified here.
- No 2027 theme yet.

## National Dolphin Day (India), 5 October: designation, national aquatic animal, Project Dolphin, survey

### Takeaway
The standing committee of the National Board for Wildlife (NBWL) decided in March 2022 that 5 October would be National Dolphin Day, starting that year. The Gangetic river dolphin was named national aquatic animal in 2009 and notified in May 2010. The first nationwide riverine dolphin estimate, released by the PM on 3 Mar 2025, counted 6,327 dolphins.

### Cited Findings
- 5 October has been National Dolphin Day since 2022, after a decision at the NBWL standing committee's 67th meeting in March 2022. — [SANDRP, 6 Oct 2025](https://sandrp.in/2025/10/06/national-dolphin-day-2025-deaths-threats-conservation-efforts-related-to-gangetic-dolphins/)
- The Environment Minister announced that 5 October would be celebrated as National Dolphin Day "from this year" (2022). — [ThePrint](https://theprint.in/india/oct-5-to-be-celebrated-as-national-dolphin-day-from-this-year-environment-minister/888393); [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/schools/oct-5-to-be-celebrated-as-national-dolphin-day-from-this-year-environment-minister-380707)
- National aquatic animal: commonly dated 5 Oct 2009. SANDRP cites the MoEF notification of May 2010. — [SANDRP](https://sandrp.in/2025/10/06/national-dolphin-day-2025-deaths-threats-conservation-efforts-related-to-gangetic-dolphins/); [IAS Gyan](https://www.iasgyan.in/daily-current-affairs/national-dolphin-day)
- First riverine dolphin estimate: 6,327 dolphins across 28 rivers in 8 states, with 3,150 man-days and more than 8,500 km surveyed. It was released by the PM at the 7th NBWL meeting at Sasan Gir on 3 Mar 2025. Uttar Pradesh had the most, followed by Bihar, West Bengal and Assam. — [AIR News, 3 Mar 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/india-has-over-6300-river-dolphins-says-first-ever-estimation-report)
- Species split given in secondary sources: 6,324 Ganges river dolphins and 3 Indus river dolphins (in Punjab's Beas). The range is given as 5,977–6,688 by one source and 5,980–6,691 by the WII brochure. — [WII river dolphin brochure](https://wii.gov.in/uploads/media/pdf/river_dolphin_brochure.pdf); [AIR News, 17 Mar 2025](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/indias-first-ever-comprehensive-river-dolphin-survey-estimated-presence-of-6327-river-dolphins-across-the-country/)
- A second estimate under Project Dolphin has started. It adds new stretches and covers Irrawaddy dolphins in the Sundarbans and Odisha. — [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/india/india-begins-recounting-its-dolphins-expands-scope-1503542270.html)

### Inferences
- The brief says the day was "declared by MoEFCC in 2022". It is more precise to say it was decided by the NBWL standing committee (March 2022) and announced by the Environment Minister.
- 5 October 2027 is a Tuesday.

### Gaps
- I did not open the PIB release for the 2022 designation (PRID 1809800 is cited by aggregators) or the 2010 notification.
- I found no primary source for Project Dolphin's launch date (announced 15 Aug 2020 and approved later; not verified here).
- No result from the second dolphin survey had been found.

## Kukur Tihar: 2026 date and expected 2027 date

### Takeaway
In Nepal, Kukur Tihar 2026 was Sunday 8 November 2026 (Kartik 22, 2083 BS). It fell on the same day as Laxmi Puja because the Chaturdashi and Aunsi (Amavasya) tithis both fell within that solar day. The expected 2027 date is Thursday 28 October 2027 (Kartik Krishna Chaturdashi, about Kartik 11 or 12, 2084 BS), from Drik Panchang. Hamro Patro or the Nepal Calendar Determination Committee should confirm it closer to the date.

### Cited Findings
- AIR News (8 Nov 2026, 7:22 PM): Nepal celebrated Kukur Tihar "today". Quote: "This year, the celebrations are even more special as Kukur Tihar coincides with Laxmi Puja." — [AIR News, 8 Nov 2026](https://newsonair.gov.in/nepal-celebrates-kukur-tihar-as-part-of-five-day-tihar-festival/)
- Spotlight Nepal (published 7 Nov 2026): Kag Tihar was observed on the day of publication. Quote: "This year however, Laxmi Puja and Kukur Tihar fall on same day." The body text also says "starts from today November 13", which contradicts its own publication date and looks like leftover copy. — [Spotlight Nepal, 7 Nov 2026](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2026/11/07/kag-tihar-2026-yampanchak-begins/)
- Merokalam: Chaturdashi and Amavasya both fell within Kartik 22, so Kukur Tihar was by day and Laxmi Puja in the evening. — [Merokalam, Tihar 2083](https://merokalam.com/blog/tihar/)
- Drik Panchang (Nepali Tihar calendar 2026), with internal inconsistencies: its table lists Kag Tihar on Fri 6 Nov, Kukur Tihar and Gai Tihar on Sun 8 Nov (Chaturdashi), Goru Tihar on 9 Nov and Bhai Tika on Tue 10 Nov. Its summary places Kukur Puja on Day 2. The Nepal news reports above put Kag Tihar on 7 Nov. — [Drik Panchang 2026](https://www.drikpanchang.com/nepali/tihar/tihar-calendar.html?year=2026)
- Conflict: one Hindutone guide gave Kukur Tihar as Sat 7 Nov 2026. Same-day Nepali news (AIR) contradicts it. — [Hindutone](https://hindutone.com/festivals/diwali-celebrations-in-kathmandu-nepal-2026-a-complete-guide/)
- Drik Panchang (Nepali Tihar calendar 2027): Kag Tihar Wed 27 Oct (Trayodashi), **Kukur Tihar Thu 28 Oct 2027 (Chaturdashi; "Kukur Puja, Narak Chaturdashi")**, Gai Tihar and Laxmi Puja Fri 29 Oct (Aunsi), Goru Tihar, Mha Puja and Nepal Sambat new year Sat 30 Oct, Bhai Tika Sun 31 Oct. — [Drik Panchang 2027](https://www.drikpanchang.com/nepali/tihar/tihar-calendar.html?year=2027)
- Vedicmarga maps the dates inconsistently: its 28 Oct 2027 page says Kartik 11, 2084 BS, but its month grid shows Kukur Tihar on Kartik 12. — [Vedicmarga 28 Oct 2027](https://vedicmarga.com/hindu-calendar/2027/10/28/); [Vedicmarga Oct 2027](https://vedicmarga.com/hindu-calendar/2027/10/?view=list)
- Laxmi Puja on 29 Oct 2027 is also given by holiday listings. — [days.to Tihar 2027](https://days.to/when-is/tihar/2027); [publicholidays.asia](https://publicholidays.asia/?p=2083)

### Inferences
- 2026 came late (8 Nov) because 2026 had an extra (adhik) lunar month. 2027 has none, so Tihar moves back about 11 days to late October.
- Darjeeling and Sikkim follow the same Hindu lunar date (Narak Chaturdashi, the day before Lakshmi Puja or Diwali). In 2027 that also points to 28 Oct. In India, Diwali or Lakshmi Puja 2027 is about 29 Oct. Local panchangs can move Chaturdashi by a day where the tithi crosses sunrise, as happened in 2026.
- Safe wording for 2027 pages: "Kukur Tihar is expected on Thursday 28 October 2027 (to be confirmed by the Nepali calendar)."

### Gaps
- I could not fetch Hamro Patro or the official Nepal Calendar Determination Committee (Nepal Panchanga Nirnayak Samiti) listing for 2026 or 2027. The BS day number for 2027 (Kartik 11 or 12) is unresolved.
- I found no Darjeeling- or Sikkim-specific source for the 2026 observance date.

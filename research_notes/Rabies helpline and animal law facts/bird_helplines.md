# Injured-bird rescue helplines: Mumbai, Delhi, Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Jaipur (as of Nov 2026)

All "seen" dates in these notes are 18 Nov 2026, when the sources below were searched or fetched. Two levels of confirmation apply. **Fetched** means the page itself was opened and read. **Search** means the number came from a search-result summary or a third-party listing and the page was not opened. Nothing here was checked by calling the number. No number in these notes is labelled as a private individual's. A few NGO lines are mobile-format numbers, which is normal for Indian NGOs, and they are flagged where it matters.

## Government and forest-department lines per city (and is 1962 used for birds?)

### Takeaway
1926 is the forest-department wildlife number. It is confirmed for Maharashtra (Mumbai), Gujarat (Ahmedabad) and Karnataka (Bengaluru). In Rajasthan (Jaipur) it was only announced in Jan 2026, and no launch was found. Delhi's forest line found is 1800118600, date not confirmed. 1962 is confirmed for injured birds only in Gujarat, as the Karuna Animal Ambulance. No source was found that ties 1962 to birds in Maharashtra, Delhi, Karnataka or Rajasthan.

### Inferences
- 1926 is the national forest-wildlife short code. It is safe to print for Maharashtra, Gujarat and Karnataka. For Rajasthan, print it only with a caveat, or after a test call.
- In Gujarat, 1962 (Karuna ambulance) is explicitly for birds. Elsewhere 1962 is the national mobile veterinary unit number, but no source found ties it to birds in the other four cities, so it should not be printed for those cities without confirmation.
- The Karuna Abhiyan WhatsApp 83200 02000 and the round-the-clock framing are tied to the January drive (10–20 Jan 2026). The page should present them as "during Uttarayan / Karuna Abhiyan".

### Gaps
- No official Delhi Forest Department page confirming 1800118600 or a 1926 line for Delhi.
- No confirmation that Rajasthan's 1926 went live after Jan 2026.
- Whether 1962 takes bird calls in Maharashtra, Delhi, Karnataka or Rajasthan: no source found.
- The Karnataka 1926 figure comes from a Deccan Herald listing of unconfirmed date. No Karnataka Forest Department page was found.
